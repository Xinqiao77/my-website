Mission & values

What guides our work

Mission: to develop protein-based approaches that act against cancer cells while sparing healthy ones, and to share the rigour behind that work with others.

Rigour

We design experiments to find out what is true, not what we hope is true.

Care

We never lose sight of the patients our research is ultimately for.

Openness
