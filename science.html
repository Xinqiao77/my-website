Science

The BSP Protein Platform

A research-stage program exploring proteins that act against cancer cells while sparing normal cells.

The challenge

Selectivity is the hardest problem in cancer therapy

Cancer cells come from the body’s own cells, so they share most of their machinery with healthy tissue. Treatments that hit cancer hard often hit healthy cells too, and that damage limits how well treatment can work.

Our research focuses on the narrow window where a therapy can harm cancer cells without harming the normal cells around them.

How it works

From protein to selective effect

A simplified view of the question each of our studies is built to answer.

  1. BSP proteins

    We design and study BSP proteins, a family of engineered proteins at the centre of our research.

  2. Selective recognition

    In laboratory studies, BSP proteins are designed to act on features that distinguish cancer cells from normal cells.

  3. Cancer cells affected, normal cells spared

    Our preclinical work measures cancer-cell death alongside the viability of healthy cells, the balance that matters most.
