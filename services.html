Services

Research services

The expertise behind our own BSP program, available to research groups and biotech companies.

Protein Research

From protein to evidence

We design, produce and characterise proteins, and test them in cell-based models with an emphasis on selectivity.

  • Protein expression and purification
  • Biochemical and biophysical characterisation
  • Cell viability and cytotoxicity assays across cancer and normal cell lines
  • Assay development and optimisation

Typical deliverables

Study design, raw and processed data, and a written report with methods and conclusions.

Data Analysis

From raw data to clear decisions

We turn experimental data into results you can trust and explain, using reproducible methods.

  • Statistical analysis and experimental design
  • Dose-response modelling and selectivity metrics
  • Bioinformatics and omics data analysis
  • Publication-ready figures and interactive dashboards

Typical deliverables

Documented analysis code, figures, and a plain-language summary of what the data does and doesn’t show.

Strategic Planning

From science to a credible plan

We help early-stage teams decide what to do next, and explain it convincingly to funders and partners.

  • R&D roadmaps and milestone planning
  • Target and indication prioritisation
  • Competitive and scientific landscape reviews
  • Grant, partnership and investor preparation

Typical deliverables

A roadmap document, decision memos, and presentation materials for your stakeholders.
